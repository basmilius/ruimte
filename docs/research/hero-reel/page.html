      The reel

      Nothing on the shortlist yet. Star a take to keep it here.

      How to choose. Put a take in the fitting room and look at it beside the headline for a while: a hero is seen a thousand times, so the one that still feels calm on the tenth loop wins. Tell Claude the number, and it goes onto the site in place of the current visuals.

      How they are made. Every take draws into the same 560 by 500 frame on the site's own tokens: ground #0d0d10, accent #155dfc, the status colors, Geist, JetBrains Mono and Kalam. Tiles run only while they are on screen, and all of them stop when your system asks for less motion.
