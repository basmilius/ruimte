# Keyboard shortcuts

<p className="lead">The shortcuts you use most, in the keys of your own keyboard. Settings, Keyboard lists them all, by area, and lets you change most of them.</p>

Typing never sets off a shortcut. Keys without a modifier work only where nothing takes text: the canvas while no node has the keyboard, a drawing's tools, the arrows, Space and Enter in a question card, and <Shortcut keys="Space" /> for Quick Look in the Files panel on a Mac.

The tables below are the defaults. If you changed a shortcut, Settings, Keyboard, the menus and the command palette show your key.

## Change a shortcut

1. Open Settings, Keyboard and pick the area, or search for the action.
2. Click the keys of the row. The row asks for a shortcut.
3. Press the new keys. Escape stops without changing anything.

A shortcut needs <Shortcut keys="Mod" />, <Shortcut keys="Ctrl" /> or <Shortcut keys="Alt" />, so a letter, a function key or Shift on its own is refused. If another action already uses the keys, the row names it. Change that action first, or press other keys. On the web, the keys your browser keeps, such as <Shortcut keys="Mod+W" /> and <Shortcut keys="Mod+T" />, are refused too.

A changed row has a button that sets it back to its default, and Restore defaults sets them all back after you confirm. If an extension uses the same keys, the row says so, and Ruimte's shortcut runs first.

Your shortcuts belong to the device and the browser you set them on. They do not follow your account. The rows with plain keys, such as a drawing's tools, the terminal keys and the browser's back and forward, show their keys but cannot be changed.
