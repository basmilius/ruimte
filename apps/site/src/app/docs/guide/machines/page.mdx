# Your account and machines

<p className="lead">Sign in to a Ruimte account, and every computer you sign in on becomes a machine you can open from anywhere: the desktop app, the web and your iPhone.</p>

## A machine

A machine is a computer that runs your projects, terminals and agents. The desktop app runs one on the computer it is installed on, unless you chose **Only connect to my machines** at the first start. A server or a computer without a screen runs one with `npx ruimte`; see [A machine without a screen](/docs/guide/headless).

## Sign in

Open Settings, Account and sign in. The machine of this computer joins your account by itself. Do the same on your other computers, and each one shows up under Machines on every device you sign in on.

A machine belongs to one account at a time, and only you reach it. To move a machine to another account, take it off its account on that machine first.

## Open another machine

The start screen lists your machines under **Machines**. Pick one, then a project on it, and it opens like a local project: its terminals, chats, files, git and launches all run on that machine. You can also browse another machine's folders from the command palette.

How the app reaches a machine, from fast to slow:

| Route | When |
| --- | --- |
| This machine | The machine runs on the computer in front of you. |
| Local network | Both are on the same network or the same tailnet. |
| Through your account | Anywhere else, through Ruimte's connection service. |
| Via relay | When a network blocks a direct route, the connection goes through a relay. It is slower. |

Settings, Account shows the route of each machine. **Reachable from other devices** checks whether your other devices can reach this machine and says what to fix if they cannot, such as a firewall or a VPN that blocks the way.

On a machine's page you can also turn off the local network route, or use another connection service. With the connection service off, only devices on its own network reach the machine.

## When a machine does not answer

A sleeping or offline machine shows as **not answering**. Ruimte keeps trying, and opens the project again once the machine is back. Nothing on the machine stops because the connection dropped.

## Updates

The desktop app updates itself. After an update, the machine on that computer still runs the old version until it restarts. Ruimte asks whether to **Restart now**, which ends the terminals and agents running there, or **When idle**, which waits until no agent works.

## Your account

Settings, Account lists your machines and the ways you sign in. **Sign out** takes this device off. **Delete account** removes the account and every sign-in to it; your machines keep their projects.
