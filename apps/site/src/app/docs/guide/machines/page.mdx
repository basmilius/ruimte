# Your account and machines

<p className="lead">Sign in to a Ruimte account, and each computer you sign in on becomes a machine you can open from the desktop app, the web and your iPhone.</p>

## Machines

A machine runs your projects, terminals and agents. The desktop app runs one on its computer, unless you chose **Only connect to my machines** at the first start. On a server or a computer without a screen, `npx ruimte` runs one; see [A machine without a screen](/docs/guide/headless).

## Sign in

1. Open Settings, Account.
2. Sign in.

This computer's machine joins your account. Sign in on your other computers too, and each machine shows up under **Machines** on every device you sign in on.

A machine belongs to one account at a time, and only you can open it. To move a machine to another account, take it off its account on that machine first.

## Open another machine

1. On the start screen, click a machine under **Machines**.
2. Pick a project on it.

<StartScreen />

The project opens like a local one. Its terminals, chats, files, git and launches run on that machine. To browse another machine's folders, choose **Browse another machine** in the command palette.

Settings, Account shows how you reach each machine:

| Route | When |
| --- | --- |
| On this machine | The machine runs on the computer in front of you. |
| Local network | Both are on the same network or tailnet. |
| Through your account | Anywhere else, through Ruimte's connection service. |
| Via relay | A network blocks the direct way. Slower. |

**Reachable from other devices**, on a machine's page, checks whether your other devices can reach it and says what to fix, such as a firewall or a VPN. The same page can close the local network route or use another connection service. With the connection service off, only devices on the machine's own network reach it.

## When a machine does not answer

A sleeping or offline machine shows as **not answering**. Ruimte keeps trying. Nothing on the machine stops because the connection dropped.

## Update a machine

The desktop app updates itself. After an update, its machine runs the old version until it restarts. Ruimte asks you to choose:

| Choice | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Restart now | Restarts the machine and ends the terminals and agents running on it |
| When idle | Restarts once no terminal or agent on it is busy |

Until the machine restarts, an orange mark sits at the foot of the rail. Click it to get the same choice again, with what runs on the machine now.

## Manage your account

Settings, Account lists your machines and your ways to sign in. **Sign out** takes this device off. **Delete account** removes the account and every sign-in to it; your machines keep their projects.
