# Needs you

<p className="lead">Ruimte lists every agent that waits for an answer, across views, projects and devices.</p>

<NeedsYou />

## Where you see it

- **Node and tab headers.** A terminal or chat that waits reads **Needs you**.
- **The sidebar.** **Needs you**, above the views, lists what waits, oldest first. To include your other open projects, turn on Settings, General, Needs you across projects.
- **The dock.** Under a canvas, the dock counts what waits and what works.
- **The canvas.** Questions and approvals of the canvas's agents stack above the dock. Step through them, fold them, or click a title to go to its node.

Answer a chat's request right in its card. For a terminal agent, the card has **Go to terminal**, since you answer in the terminal.

## Jump to what waits

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| <Shortcut keys="Mod+Alt+P" /> | Go to the next node that needs you, in any view, oldest first |
| <Shortcut keys="Mod+Alt+Shift+P" /> | Open the attention queue: what waits, and turns that finished while you were away |
| <Shortcut keys="Mod+Shift+P" /> | Move the keyboard to the questions and approvals on the canvas |

## Snooze an agent

Hover a waiting agent and click the snooze button. Choose **10 minutes**, **1 hour** or **Tomorrow**. The agent leaves the list and raises nothing until the snooze ends.

## Notifications

Ruimte notifies you when an agent needs you or finishes. Turn its sound on or off under Settings, Agents, Notification sound.

With the iPhone app, a notification reaches your phone only once you step away from your computer. A question waits a minute and an approval ten seconds. If you answer in that time, your phone stays quiet. See [Phone and web](/docs/guide/phone-and-web).

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are hours in which a device raises no alert. Agents keep working, and what still waits is announced when the hours end. Each device has its own hours, and they are off until you turn them on.

On your computer, turn on Settings, Agents, Quiet hours, then pick the days and the hours. Desktop notifications stop. The lists, counts and marks still show what waits, so opening Ruimte at night shows the truth.

On your iPhone, open Settings, Notifications, Quiet hours. Your machines send nothing to the phone in those hours, except a notification an agent sent you on purpose. A question, an approval or a failed task waits. When the hours end, the phone gets one notification for each agent that still needs you. A finished turn is not repeated, and neither is anything you answered in the meantime. If a machine restarts during the hours, it forgets what it held, and the agent still waits in the app. A machine that is too old for quiet hours says so under the section and keeps notifying.

The hours follow the clock and time zone of the device. A night belongs to the day it starts on, so Friday from 22:00 to 07:00 runs into Saturday morning. An end at or before the start runs into the next day, and the same time twice covers a whole day.

## Break reminder

In the desktop app, turn on Settings, Agents, Break reminder and pick a stretch from 30 minutes to 2 hours. It starts at 50. Ruimte counts the time you are at the computer, in any app. When the stretch is up, a **Time for a break?** card offers **Take a break** and **Later**. Without a Ruimte window in front, a notification says the same, and a click opens the card. **Later** asks again after 15 minutes.

Five minutes away from the computer counts as a break and starts the stretch over. The reminder stays silent in your quiet hours. It is for this computer only: it reaches no phone and no other machine, and the web version does not have it.

## Keep the computer awake

A computer that sleeps pauses every agent on it. Set Settings, Agents, Keep awake:

| Choice | The computer stays awake |
| --- | --- |
| Off | Never; it follows your system settings |
| While agents work | Only while an agent works |
| Always | Also when no agent works |

Below it, turn on **Also on battery**, **Keep display on** and, on a Mac, **Also with the lid closed**. The lid setting asks once for an administrator password.
