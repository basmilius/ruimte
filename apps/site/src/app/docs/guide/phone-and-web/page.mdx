# Phone and web

<p className="lead">Open your machines from any browser at station.ruimte.app, or follow and answer your agents from an iPhone or iPad.</p>

## Station, the web app

[station.ruimte.app](https://station.ruimte.app) is Ruimte in a browser. Sign in with your account and your machines are there, with the same projects, canvases, terminals and chats as in the desktop app. Nothing runs in the browser itself. Every project lives on one of your machines.

A few things differ from the desktop app:

- A browser keeps some shortcuts for itself, such as `Cmd+W`, `Cmd+T` and `Cmd+N`. Their commands still work from the menu.
- A web page in a browser node is shown by the machine, once you turn on Browser and device streaming for it under Settings, Account.
- Files from your computer only drop onto a canvas in the desktop app. Open them from the Files panel instead.

## The iPhone and iPad app

The app connects to your machines and runs nothing of its own. It opens projects, chats, terminals, files, drawings, diagrams and devices.

On an iPhone, five tabs hold everything:

- **Now** gathers your open projects across machines into Needs you, Working, Finished and Coming up. Answer a question or approval right on its card.
- **Projects** opens a project and its views.
- **Routines** lists the routines of every machine.
- **Machines** shows each machine, with its updates and keep awake.
- **Search** looks through your machines.

An iPad shows the same in a sidebar, with files, git and sub-agents in an inspector beside the page. `Cmd+K` opens search.

A terminal on the phone follows the size the desktop gave it. A launch that waits for approval shows its command, folder and environment, and runs only after you tap **Launch**. Widgets on the Home Screen show what needs you.

## Notifications on your phone

Each device picks which notifications it gets: an agent that needs you (the default), a finished turn, or a process warning, for all projects or a few.

A notification waits while you sit at a computer with Ruimte, so your phone stays quiet while you work. Once you step away, it goes out. Snooze an agent and it raises nothing until the snooze ends, also when you set the snooze offline.
