# Phone and web

<p className="lead">Open your machines in a browser at station.ruimte.app, or follow and answer your agents on an iPhone or iPad.</p>

## Station

Go to [station.ruimte.app](https://station.ruimte.app) and sign in. Your machines and their projects open as in the desktop app. Nothing runs in the browser; every project lives on one of your machines.

Station differs from the desktop app in a few places:

- The browser keeps some shortcuts for itself, such as <Shortcut keys="Mod+W" />, <Shortcut keys="Mod+T" /> and <Shortcut keys="Mod+N" />. Use the menu for those commands.
- A browser node shows its page only when **Browser and device streaming** is on for its machine, under Settings, Account.
- Files from your computer do not drop onto a canvas. Open them from the Files panel.

## The iPhone and iPad app

The app connects to your machines and runs nothing itself. It opens projects, chats, terminals, files, drawings, diagrams and devices.

On an iPhone, five tabs hold everything:

<PhoneNow />

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Now | Your open projects on every machine, grouped into Needs you, Working, Finished and Coming up. Answer a question or approval on its card. |
| Projects | Your projects and their views |
| Routines | The routines of every machine |
| Machines | Each machine, with its updates and keep awake |
| Search | Search |

On an iPad the same pages sit in a sidebar, with files and git in an inspector. <Shortcut keys="Mod+K" /> opens Search.

- A terminal keeps the size the desktop gave it.
- A launch that waits for approval shows its command, folder and environment. It runs after you tap **Launch**.
- Home Screen widgets show what needs you.

## Notifications on your phone

Under **Notify me when**, each device picks **An agent needs you**, **A turn finishes** or **A process misbehaves**, for all projects or some.

While you use a computer with Ruimte, notifications wait. Once you step away, they reach your phone. A snoozed agent raises nothing until the snooze ends, also when you set the snooze offline.
