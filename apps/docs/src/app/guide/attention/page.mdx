# Needs you

<p className="lead">With several agents at work, the hard part is knowing which one waits for you. Ruimte keeps that list for you, across views, projects and devices.</p>

## Status at a glance

Every terminal and chat with an agent shows a status in its header and beside its row in the sidebar. **Needs you** means the agent waits on a question or a permission. A tab in a grid cell shows the same.

The dock under a canvas shows how many agents are working and how many wait. Hover it to read what each one asks.

## The Needs you list

The sidebar lists everything that waits, oldest first, with what each agent asks: a command it wants to run, a file it wants to edit, or its question. Settings, General, Needs you across projects adds what waits in your other open projects, grouped by project.

For an agent in a chat, answer right there in the list. For an agent in a terminal, the list takes you to the terminal, since you answer in its own interface.

## Jump to what waits

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Cmd+Option+P` | Go to the next node that needs you, in any view, oldest first |
| `Cmd+Option+Shift+P` | Open the attention queue: what waits on you, and turns that finished while you were away |
| `Cmd+Shift+P` | Move the keyboard to the questions and approvals on this canvas |

On a canvas, waiting questions and approvals stack in a card you can step through, fold, or follow to their node with **Go to node**.

## Snooze

Not now? **Snooze** a waiting agent for 10 minutes, 1 hour or until tomorrow. It leaves the list and raises nothing until the snooze ends.

## Notifications

Ruimte notifies you when an agent needs you or finishes, with a sound you choose under Settings, Agents, Notifications.

With the iPhone app, an alert goes to your phone, but only once you step away. While you use a computer with Ruimte, a question waits a minute and a permission request ten seconds before it reaches your phone. Answer it in that time and your phone stays quiet. See [Phone and web](/guide/phone-and-web).

## Keep the computer awake

When a computer sleeps, every agent on it pauses. Settings, Agents, Keep awake chooses:

- **Off**
- **While agents work**
- **Always**

You can also let it stay awake on battery, keep the display on, and keep a Mac running with its lid closed. That last one needs a one-time permission from an administrator.
