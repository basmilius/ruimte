# What Ruimte is

<p className="lead">Ruimte is a workspace for working with coding agents. Terminals, AI chats, browsers, devices, notes, files and databases sit side by side in views you switch between, and everything keeps running when you close the window.</p>

It is made for a person who runs several Claude Code or Codex sessions at once and loses track of which one is waiting. In Ruimte you see at a glance which agent works, which one is done and which one needs you.

## The pieces

A handful of words come back on every page of this guide.

**Machine.** The program that runs your terminals, agents and browsers. The desktop app starts one on your computer. A machine can also run on a server or another computer of yours, and you open its projects from anywhere you sign in.

**Project.** A folder you work in. Ruimte adds a `.ruimte` folder to it the first time you open it, and keeps the project's views there.

**View.** One page of a project, listed in the sidebar. Most views are a canvas. A terminal, a chat, a browser, a file, a drawing or a diagram can also be a view of its own.

**Canvas.** A surface where nodes sit next to each other: terminals, chats, browsers, notes, files, devices and groups. You draw lines between them to give an agent context.

**Session.** What runs inside a terminal or chat. A session lives on the machine, not in the window. Quit Ruimte halfway through a test run, open it tomorrow, and the terminal is where you left it, scrollback included.

**Agent.** A coding CLI on your machine, such as Claude Code or Codex. Ruimte starts the CLI you already installed and logged in to. An agent runs in a terminal, in its own interface, or in an AI chat that Ruimte draws.

## What it does for you

- It shows the state of every agent: running, idle, waiting for you, or stopped with an error. See [Needs you](/guide/attention).
- It answers an agent's questions and approvals in the app or on your phone, for agents in an AI chat. See [AI chats](/guide/chats).
- It lets agents open other agents, give them tasks and wake up when the work is done. A child never gets more permissions than its parent. See [Agents working together](/guide/delegation).
- It keeps a project's commands, files, git, databases and devices next to the agents that use them. See the pages under Project tools.
- It opens the projects of all your machines in one app, on the web at station.ruimte.app and on an iPhone. See [Your account and machines](/guide/machines).

## How the parts fit

The machine runs every session. The app is a window onto it, and so are the web app and the iPhone app. Several windows can show the same project at once. Closing a window ends nothing, and neither does a lost connection. Your terminals and agents stop only when you close the project in the last window that has it open.

Your code stays in your project folder. The `.ruimte` folder inside it holds the views you share with your team, so you can commit it. Your private views sit in `.ruimte/private`, which Ruimte keeps out of git.
